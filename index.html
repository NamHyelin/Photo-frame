Four-Frame Studio

Set up your iPad and automatically take four photos with a countdown.

01 · Your Frame

A transparent PNG frame works best. The frame is fitted over the entire finished image. Use a frame with four openings for the photos.
Using the default white frame

02 · Photo Booth 0 / 4 photos

Start the camera, secure your iPad, and begin the photo session.
3
Secure your iPad and allow camera access in Safari. Auto capture takes four photos in sequence after the countdown. Photos are processed on your device.

03 · Preview & Save

Tap Save / Share, then choose Save Image or Save to Photos in the share menu. If unavailable, the file will download.

04 · Decorate Your Photo

Create your photo strip first. Drag with one finger to move an image; pinch with two fingers to resize, and rotate two fingers to change its angle. Added images appear above the photo. Transparent PNG stickers work best.
