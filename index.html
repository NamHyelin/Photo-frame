Step 1 — Choose Your Frame

Choose a frame image from your photo library. Move or resize the fixed 9:16 crop box, then confirm your frame.

Your selected frame will appear here.
The crop box always stays at 9:16. Drag inside it to move; drag a corner handle to resize. The cropped image becomes your final frame.

Step 2 — Take Your Photos

The front camera will take four photos automatically. Get ready when the countdown appears.

Camera preview will appear here.
Allow camera access when prompted.

Step 3 — Decorate & Export

Your four photos are arranged in a 2 × 2 layout. Add stickers and use one finger to move them, or two fingers to resize and rotate.

Photo placement: upper 59% of the frame, with a small gap between images. The lower area is left open for frame artwork or text.
Tip: transparent PNG stickers work best. Tap a sticker to select it. Drag to move; pinch to resize; twist to rotate. Use Delete Sticker to remove the selected item.
Save or AirDrop opens the iPad share sheet when supported.
